## Problem Statement
Organic food demand is rising, but fake certificates and limited transparency reduce consumer trust. The project addresses the need for a reliable way to verify organic certification and trace product history across the supply chain.

## Proposed Solution
OFTS is a blockchain-based traceability platform that integrates smart contracts, QR codes, and optional IoT data collection. Producers register batches and upload certification documents; certifiers review and approve or reject them. Approved batches generate QR codes that consumers can scan to view verified traceability records.

## Core Technologies
- Blockchain for immutable traceability records and certification decisions.
- Smart contracts to automate verification workflows.
- QR codes to connect physical products with digital traceability data.
- IPFS to store large certification documents off-chain while keeping on-chain hashes.
- Future IoT integration to record environmental conditions during transport and storage.

## Key Roles
Producer, Certifier, Distributor, Retailer, and Consumer.

## Primary Use Cases
- Create product batch and generate a unique Batch ID.
- Upload certification documents to off-chain storage with on-chain hashes.
- Review and approve or reject certifications.
- Record supply chain events (receiving, storing, transferring, selling).
- Generate QR codes for certified batches.
- Scan QR codes to retrieve traceability history.
- View complete traceability data across the supply chain.

## Outcomes
The report concludes that OFTS can increase transparency, reduce fraud, and improve confidence in organic food by providing tamper-proof traceability records accessible to all supply-chain participants and consumers.
